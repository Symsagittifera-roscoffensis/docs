## I. Summary

Engineering generally involves separate people working on their own projects.
This proposal aims to get engineering to work together on a single project *every* shift.

## II. Problem

### Main Gameplay

Currently Engineering gameplay falls into a five main categories:
- Initial setup of distro and power.
- Repairs from meteors, explosions et cetera.
- Individual projects (e.g. making ploux, LOX, more fancy reactor/SM).
- Teaching new people: The setup of an engine or other contraption is generally taken much slower and will be much more RP focused.
- The Chief Engineer or otherwise proposes a project that the department works collaboratively on. 

In my experience most engineering gameplay ends up falling into the third category or become tiders.
This is especially on rounds without major disruption with antags as most station maintenance is a result of this.

Individual projects usually have very little round impact. 

### Incident response

Engineers currently don't have many reasons to get called out during a shift. Currently they are almost all one of the following:

- Fixing spacing from meteors
- Fixing Artifact Research
- Adding scrubbers to botany
- Fixing Antagonist Damage

The first three of these aren't typically that interesting, you can only play out the "science blew themselves up again" bit out so many times.
Fixing antagonist Damage can have more interesting interactions but antagonists have been removed.

Furthermore, the majority of incident response is generally travel time between engineering and the incident, which will often require two round trips if an engineer didn't have the materials required for a repair.

### Chief Engineering

Currently, Chief Engineer is a role that is one of the least wanted Command roles.
Station Engineers will generally decline promotions to Acting Chief Engineer and when they don't this is generally so they can get CE gear which is currently considerably better than base engineer gear.
- They have very little agency over their department as station engineers will typically be engaging in their own projects - making it almost impossible for CEs to delegate tasks to their department.
- Because of the above, Chief Engineers will often be forced to be the ones to respond to the incidents for engineering.

### Learning *Fancy* Engineering

Learning many aspects of engineering is currently very difficult:
- HFR isn't even in the guidebook.
- SM guidebook doesn't list doesn't give details for most of the interactions.
- Active tesla isn't mentioned in the guidebook.
- Mechanics for different materials for reactor rods aren't mentioned in the guidebook

Whilst learning and teaching these mechanics while playing is rewarding, telling players how things work in the guidebook doesn't take a way from that.
Technical assistents are still taught things in the guidebook but things not in the guidebook end up not been taught much if at all.

Learning these mechanics ends up turning engineering into a roguelike where you see how far you can get into learning a mechanic - whilst fighting off calls to deal with other things on the station - before the round ends and you have to start again.

## III. Intent

If this proposal is successful engineering should be:
- Working together to achieve a project that takes most of the round to achieve.
- Said project should be visible to the rest of the crew; whether that be through windows or otherwise.
- This project should be completed most but not all of the time (currently unsure what this success rate should be tuned to - if it needs tuning at all).
- Solo engineering endeavours should be significantly less common, if they occur at all.
- Chief Engineers should be acting much more as a management position.

## IV. Solution

The underlying change of this proposal is for engineering to engage on a joint and collaborative project every shift.
This would likely have to be enforced through a modification to SOP.
The Chief Engineer would be responsible for ensuring the project is of the correct scale given the experience and size of the engineering team.

At round start Engineering should have time to do a briefing similar to security.
Most round start setup of engineering should be removed.

### Gas Miners & Distro/Waste

The station would start with atmospheric distribution and waste already setup.
This setup should include heating/cooling and should recycle o2 and n2. 

Gas miners should be removed.
The main way to get additional gases like plasma should be through engines or being purchased.
This discourages solo engineering projects.

Dealing with waste gases becomes much more important as you don't just get more for free.

### Visability

Engineering rooms should be made more visible to the rest of the crew - similar to the AME room on Core.
It should be possible for the majority of crew to observe what the engineering department is doing.

### Shared blueprints

I do not know the viability of this but making engineers construction blueprints be shared between all engineers would aid greatly in collaboration.
