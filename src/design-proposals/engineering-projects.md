## I. Summary

Engineering generally involves separate people working on their own projects.
This proposal aims to get engineering to work together on a single project *every* shift.

## II. Problem

Currently Engineering tasks fall into a three main categories:
1. Initial setup of distro and power.
2. Repairs from meteors, explosions et cetera.
3. Individual projects (e.g. making ploux, LOX, more fancy reactor/SM).

Most of an engineer's round will either fall into the third category.
This typically leads to Station Engineer being a very low interaction role.


## III. Intent

If this proposal is successful engineering should be:
- Working together to achieve a project that takes most of the round to achieve.
- Said project should be visible to the rest of the crew; whether that be through windows or otherwise.
- This project should be completed most but not all of the time (currently unsure what this success rate should be tuned to - if it needs tuning at all).
- Solo engineering endeavours should be significantly less common, if they occur at all.

## IV. Solution

The underlying change of this proposal is for engineering to engage on a joint and collaborative project every shift.
This would likely have to be enforced through a modification to SOP.
The Chief Engineer would be responsible for ensuring the project is of the correct scale given the experience and size of the engineering team.

At round start Engineering should have time to do a briefing similar to security.
Most round start setup of engineering should be removed.

### Gas Miners & Distro/Waste

The station would start with atmospheric distribution and waste already setup.
This setup should include heating/cooling and should recycle o2 and n2. 

Gas miners should be removed.
The main way to get additional gases like plasma should be through engines or being purchased.
This discourages solo engineering projects.


### Visability

Engineering rooms should be made more visible to the rest of the crew - similar to the AME room on Core.
It should be possible for the majority of crew to observe what the engineering department is doing.

### New Engines

Aim for several new engines. They done have to have utility in their own right and could provide benefits other than power.
Some should involve major collaborations with other departments.
These should all be designed with the following in mind:
- It should be difficult or impossible to use the same setup for the same engine every round.
- They should encourage as much interactions with other departments as possible.
- They should be reasonable to teach the fundementals of.
- They should have utility outside of power generation if they can do that at all.
